QUALITATIVE DISCOVERY / LIFE CONTEXT

Before advising the capital, understand the person.

Long-term suitability depends on the life this portfolio must support—not only a risk score.

01 / GOALS

What major goals do you expect in the next 1, 3, and 5+ years—such as a home, wedding, car, family, or education?

02 / EMPLOYMENT

How stable is your employment and income, and do you expect any career change, unpaid leave, or relocation?

03 / HEALTH

Do you have any health conditions or dependants that may require significant financial support?

04 / PROTECTION

What life, health, disability, or critical-illness insurance do you currently have, and where might there be protection gaps?

05 / PRIORITIES

Which goals are essential, and which could be delayed if markets fall or your circumstances change?
